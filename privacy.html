Doci mail - Privacy Policy

Doci mail is a personal application. It is used only by its owner, for the owner's own Gmail accounts.

  • Access: the app requests read-only access to Gmail (gmail.readonly). It cannot send, modify or delete email.
  • Use of data: it looks for invoice attachments and saves copies of them. This is the only purpose.
  • Storage: the data stays on the owner's computer and the owner's Google Drive. No servers are operated by the developer.
  • Sharing: data is not sold, shared or transferred to any third party. It is not used for advertising or to train AI models.
  • Revoking access: access can be withdrawn at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
